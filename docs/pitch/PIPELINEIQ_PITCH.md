## The Problem

B2B sales teams in Africa and emerging markets are flying blind.

They use CRM tools — Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho — but none of those
platforms tell them what is actually happening inside their pipeline.
They log deals. They do not understand them.

The result is predictable:

- Sales leaders cannot forecast with confidence
- High-value customers churn without warning
- Lost deals repeat the same patterns nobody tracks
- Rep performance gaps go unaddressed until quota is already missed

The analytics tools that solve this problem — Clari, Gong, People.ai
— are priced for Silicon Valley enterprise budgets. A Clari licence
starts at $1,500 per user per year. For a 10-person sales team in
Lagos or Nairobi, that is not a conversation that happens.

The market is large, underserved, and ready.

## The Product

**PipelineIQ** is a CRM analytics engine that plugs into any existing
CRM and turns raw pipeline data into revenue decisions.

It answers the five questions every sales leader asks every Monday:

| Question | PipelineIQ Answer |
|----------|------------------|
| Where is revenue leaking? | Pipeline funnel with stage-by-stage value and velocity |
| Which customers will churn? | Churn score and LTV-weighted at-risk account list |
| Which reps are on track? | Quota attainment and win rate by rep, updated daily |
| What is killing our deals? | Loss reason analysis with revenue impact per root cause |
| What will we close this quarter? | Weighted pipeline forecast with rep-level drill-down |

### Core Features — Version 1.0

**Pipeline Intelligence**
Real-time funnel view showing deal count, total value, weighted
value, and average velocity at every stage. Deals stalling beyond
the stage benchmark trigger an automatic alert.

**Churn Risk Monitor**
Every account is scored daily on a 0–1 churn probability model.
Accounts cross a threshold and land on a prioritised intervention
list for the Customer Success team, ranked by LTV at risk.

**Rep Performance Scorecard**
Individual dashboards for every Account Executive showing quota
attainment, win rate, average deal size, and deal velocity —
compared against team benchmarks in real time.

**Revenue Forecasting**
Weighted pipeline projections by rep, region, and product tier.
Monthly and quarterly views with month-on-month growth tracking
and a 3-month rolling average to smooth noise.

**Loss Intelligence**
Every lost deal is categorised by root cause. PipelineIQ surfaces
the top loss drivers by deal count and revenue value, so sales
leadership can address patterns rather than individual incidents.

## Why Now

Three forces are converging:

**1. African SaaS adoption has crossed the threshold.**
Nigeria alone added 2.1 million new internet users in 2024.
Enterprise SaaS penetration in Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt
is now comparable to Tier 2 cities in Southeast Asia five years ago.
The infrastructure is there. The appetite is there.

**2. Global CRM vendors are not solving analytics for this market.**
Salesforce Einstein and HubSpot's reporting suite require data
science teams to configure. They are built for companies with
dedicated RevOps functions. Most African B2B companies have a
sales manager and a spreadsheet.

**3. The founder has solved this problem before.**
PipelineIQ was not built by a generalist. It was built by a
healthcare CIO who ran digital transformation programmes at federal
level in Nigeria, managed analytics for a pharmacy group, and holds
an MSc in Big Data and Business Intelligence. The product reflects
20 years of understanding what sales and operations leaders actually
need to see — not what analytics vendors think they need.
